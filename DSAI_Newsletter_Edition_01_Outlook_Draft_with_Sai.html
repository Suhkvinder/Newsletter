SuperNOVA
The four big bets
From announcement to build
The Big Bets are now moving from design into delivery phase. Teams are translating their initial roadmaps into practical work: bringing together the right science, data, technology and people into their teams to start planning, testing the early approaches; and learning what it will take to make each solution achieve a high impact in day-to-day R&D.
This phase is about building momentum through tangible use cases, while staying focused on the outcomes that matter most: better decisions, shorter cycles and more time for our people to focus on discovering medicines.
01
In silico biology
Biology understood before the bench.
Leads: Alix Berton · Jared Worful
The first build for a virtual CVRM patient combines dynamic heart and kidney models that bring fragmented biological evidence into a single hypothesis–simulate–test–learn loop. By year end, the target is the data foundation and an early prototype driven by at least one organ model.
What’s next: The team is prioritizing the disease area, strengthening the data foundation and developing the first simulation MVPs, then testing the approach with internal scientific teams.
02
Molecular Design Studio
Design more. Synthesize less.
Leads: Stefan Gradl · Katharina Meier
Molecular Design Studio is taking shape as a connected, AI-enabled workflow across small molecules and biologics. Building on progress in antibody design, the focus now is on linking design, make, test and analysis more seamlessly—so teams can compare options faster, learn from results sooner and improve molecule designs with greater confidence.
What’s next: Connect design data, predictive models and chemistry and biology workflows, then pilot the first studio experience with active discovery teams.
03
Predictive Trial Mastery
Trials scored before the spend.
Leads: Vidya Perera · Gurpreet Nanda
PTM connects Bayer’s clinical, biomarker, real-world, MIDD and trial evidence into one governed, stage-aware forecast. It is being reimagined, supporting Development leaders who oversee major development and trial decisions. Teams get a transparent decision view of what is known, what remains uncertain and what evidence could change the outcome.
What’s next: Build asset-level evidence views, calibrate forecasts against comparable programmes and pilot decision support with development leaders.
04
Trial Agentification
Operations at compute speed.
Leads: Jakob Kaiser · Pam Battaglia
Clinical and regulatory work reimagined as continuous, agent-orchestrated flows, with humans accountable at every decision point. The team has established the scope for a “Trial Factory” and is selecting the first controlled, high-value workflows where agents can help bring information together and prepare materials for expert review, while people remain accountable for every decision.
What’s next: Identify frontrunner workflows, assess dependencies, develop an implementation blueprint and prove value in controlled environments.

PROJECT SPOTLIGHT
TargetX: From AI Experiments to Discovery You Can Reuse
Mission Lead: Dr. Mahmoud Ibrahim, Whippney
Our team has been working on new developments for TargetX, which has converted previously isolated AI experiments into reusable, auditable capabilities and a Target Nomination Studio that combines diverse evidence to prioritise drug targets. Supported by modular, AI-ready WEBB Knowledge Graphs, this has enabled 13 targets to enter experimental validation. It supports Bayer’s rAIvolution and SuperNOVA Big Bet by widening and accelerating the discovery funnel, freeing scientists to focus on science while improving the quality and speed of early R&D decisions.
